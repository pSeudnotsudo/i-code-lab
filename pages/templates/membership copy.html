Director Spotlight

AM
Amina Mwangi
Founder & Executive Director
LEADERSHIP

Amina founded i-Code Innovation Lab with a clear goal: close the digital skills gap for Kenyan youth. With over 14 years of experience in software engineering and education policy, she has guided the lab from a small weekend workshop into a multi-programme institution serving hundreds of students each term. She holds an MSc in Computer Science from the University of Nairobi and is a fellow of the African Leadership Initiative.
