About I-CODE

What is I-CODE?

i-Code is a next-generation tech lab that trains students from childhood to career level by combining robotics, AI, and cybersecurity into a hands-on learning experience.

🚀 A Real Innovation Lab

A real innovation lab,
not just a classroom

Our students learn on professional workstations with industry-standard tools. From coding environments to robotics stations, everything is set up for hands-on building.

  • Small class sizes so every student gets personal attention from expert mentors.
  • Project-based curriculum where students build real things they can showcase.
  • Programs for all ages — from Scratch for kids to cybersecurity for professionals.
  • Certificates recognised by industry partners upon course completion.
