About the Programme

Over 3 action-packed weeks, your child will join one of three innovation tracks — Explorers, Builders, or Innovators — building real robots, writing real code, and exploring real AI tools, six hours a day. It all leads to Showcase Day, where every student presents their own project to family, mentors, and guests at our Mega Innovation Showcase. Every child leaves with a certificate of completion, a project portfolio, a personalised placement report, and a clear recommendation for their next step on the Future Innovator Pathway.

What will my child learn?
  • Coding – developing simple programs, apps & games using visual and/or text-based languages
  • Electronics and Robotics – from first principles to advanced robot programming
  • AI & Design Thinking – logical and computational thinking

Requirements
